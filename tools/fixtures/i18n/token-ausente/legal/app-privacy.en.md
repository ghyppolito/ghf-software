# Privacy Policy — App

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

## 1. Data collected

### 1.1 Location

The app asks for `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` to record the trail.

### 1.2 Notifications

The app asks for notification permission to tell you when recording ends.

## 2. Third parties

We use Firebase Analytics, by Google, without identifying you.
