## 1. About the Service

LottoExpert is a **statistical and mathematical analysis** application for Brazilian lotteries. It offers wheeling (closings and reductions) with mathematical guarantee, mathematical filters, historical backtesting, ticket checking (manual and, for Premium subscribers, automatic), frequency/delay statistics, financial tracking of user games ("My Performance"), registration of the same game across several consecutive draws ("Teimosinha"), and sweepstakes organization among participants, with a settlement statement of the prize division.
