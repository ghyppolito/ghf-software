## 1. What the App is

Pole Position is a sports telemetry application for Android, intended for kart drivers and amateur motorsport drivers. It uses the device's GPS and motion sensors to:

- time laps and splits (sectors) from finish and sector lines configured by the user;
- measure speed (GPS) and G-force;
- record, optionally and per track, the racing line of the best lap;
- keep the session history, display analyses and allow exporting (CSV) and sharing an analysis card (image).

The App works without registration, without login and without an account. Driving data is stored **only on your device**, as described in the Privacy Policy.
