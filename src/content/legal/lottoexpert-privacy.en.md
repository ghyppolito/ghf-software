# Privacy Policy — LottoExpert

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **LottoExpert** application collects, uses, and protects your information. Our fundamental commitment is to **privacy** and data security.

---

## 1. General Principle: Privacy by Default

LottoExpert is a **Privacy-First** application. All data processing—game generation, statistical analysis, ticket checking, financial performance calculation, and history storage—occurs **exclusively on your device**. No personal information, betting strategy, or tickets created by you are transmitted to any server.

The only two exceptions, both described in detail below, are: **pseudonymous usage metrics** from the analytics service, which can be disabled within the app (Section 4.7), and the **optional backup on your own Google Drive**, which only functions upon explicit authorization and to which the developer has no access (Section 4.9).

To retrieve official draw results, the app communicates with the developer's own servers hosted on Google Cloud Platform (GCP)—described in Section 4.1. These requests are anonymous and do not contain any personal data.

---

## 2. Data the App DOES NOT Collect

LottoExpert **does not collect, transmit, or store** on remote servers:

- Your selected numbers or betting strategies
- Tickets created or saved by you
- Your betting history (which games, in which draws, with which numbers)
- Identifying personal data (email, phone, tax ID/CPF)
- Images captured by the camera
- Location data

**Note on prize values:** The app relies on a usage analytics service (Firebase Analytics, Section 4.7) that records, in a **pseudonymous manner and per event**, aggregate monetary values of checked prizes — for example, the total prize amount calculated during an automatic check or in the monthly report. These values are not accompanied by numbers, personal identification, or bet amounts, and serve to measure whether checking features deliver results. You can disable this collection at any time in **About → Notifications → Privacy** (Section 4.7).

**Note on Sweepstake names:** Participant names entered in the "My Sweepstakes" module are stored exclusively in your device's local database and are never sent to our servers. These names only leave the device if and when you explicitly choose to share an invite link, share the sweepstake's settlement statement image, export a PDF, or enable Google Drive backup (see Sections 4.8 and 4.9).

**Note on Google Drive Backup (Premium):** If you explicitly enable this optional feature, a copy of your saved games, sweepstakes (including participant names), and preferences is sent to a hidden and private folder on **your own Google Drive**, controlled by you. Even in this case, **the developer does not collect, receive, or have access** to this data — it remains under your control in your Google account. Details in Section 4.9.

## 3. Data Processed Locally on the Device

The following data is stored in the app's private database (Room Database, SQLite), accessible only by the application itself:

- **Draw history:** Lottery results obtained from our data infrastructure (GCP), saved for offline consultation.
- **Saved games:** Tickets generated by the algorithm or created manually by the user.
- **Sweepstakes:** Data from groups created by the user, including participant names, quota links, and sweepstake results (total prize and hit tier).
- **Participations per draw:** The link between a saved ticket and the draw in which it was wagered, with the recorded cost at bet time and, after the draw, hit counts and prize won. This is the basis of the "My Performance" dashboard and monthly report — all financial calculation is done on device.
- **Monitored numbers and saved strategies:** Numbers selected for smart alerts and generation settings saved by the user.
- **Filter preferences:** Local UI configuration settings.

The user has full control over this data and can **manually delete it** at any time using the "Clear" or "Remove" options in the app. When **uninstalling the application**, all local data is permanently removed from the device.

## 4. Communication with External Services

For the app to function, we communicate with the following external services:

### 4.1 Developer Data Infrastructure (Google Cloud Platform)

- **Purpose:** Provide official Brazilian lottery draw results to the app.
- **Operation:** The results are periodically collected from the Caixa API via an automated developer backend process and stored in a private GCP database. The app queries this database to sync results, avoiding direct calls to Caixa's public API.
- **Data sent:** No personal data. The request includes only the lottery type and, optionally, the requested draw number. Additionally, a Firebase App Check attestation token is sent to verify that the request comes from a legitimate installation (see Section 4.2).
- **Data stored:** Only public draw results. No user data is recorded on developer servers.
- **Frequency:** Collecting results from the official source is performed hourly by the developer's automated process (approx. 08:30 AM to 11:30 PM BRT) to cover unpredictable delays in official publication. The **app** queries the infrastructure in four daily windows (approx. 08:30 AM, 12:30 PM, 10:30 PM, and 11:35 PM BRT, with random device jitter), in addition to manual user updates.
- **Infrastructure Provider:** Google Cloud Platform. Subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4.2 Firebase App Check (Google)

- **Purpose:** Guarantee that only legitimate LottoExpert installations can access the developer's data infrastructure (Section 4.1), protecting against abusive API use by unauthorized applications.
- **Operation:** App Check uses **Google Play Integrity** to verify device and app integrity, creating an attestation token sent with sync requests. This token does not contain personal data, does not identify the user, and is not stored by the developer.
- **Data processed by Google:** Device integrity assessment is handled by Google Play Integrity. LottoExpert only accesses the validity confirmation.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Play Integrity Terms](https://developer.android.com/google/play/integrity).

### 4.3 Google Play Billing

- **Purpose:** Process in-app purchases and subscriptions to unlock Premium features. The app offers three options: monthly subscription, annual subscription, and one-time lifetime purchase.
- **Data processed by Google:** Payment details and purchase history are managed entirely by Google Play. LottoExpert only receives encrypted confirmation to unlock Premium features—without access to card details, payment methods, or user financial information.
- **Subscription status notifications:** Google Play sends automated notifications to developer infrastructure regarding changes in subscription status (renewal, cancellation, expiration). This flow is described in Section 4.10.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

### 4.4 Firebase Remote Config (Google)

- **Purpose:** Keep minimum bet prices updated without the need to update the application. The values are read by the app to display cost estimates to the user.
- **Data sent:** No personal data. The request includes only anonymous technical metadata from the app (app identifier and version), used by Firebase for request rate control.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4.5 Google Play In-App Review

- **Purpose:** Display the native Google Play dialog box requesting an app review after a positive experience (e.g., checking a winning ticket).
- **Data processed by Google:** The review flow is managed entirely by Google Play. LottoExpert does not collect or view the content or rating of the submitted review.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

### 4.6 Firebase Crashlytics (Google)

- **Purpose:** Detect and record technical failures (crashes) of the application for diagnosis and correction by the developer.
- **Data sent:** Anonymous stack trace of the failure, app version, device type, and Android version. **No personal data is transmitted:** we do not collect user identifiers, game data, strategies, or any information entered by the user.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4.7 Firebase Analytics (Google)

- **Purpose:** Measure aggregate and anonymous usage of features to improve the user experience, guide development, and—in strictly aggregate form, without individual identification—optimize developer advertising campaigns on platforms like Google Ads and Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram). No individual data is shared with these platforms; use is restricted to aggregate conversion metrics (e.g., total number of users who made a purchase) to statistically calibrate ad audiences.
- **Data sent:** Pseudonymous and aggregate usage events. Examples of events collected:
    - *Feature usage:* `screen_view`, `generate_matrix`, `scan_ocr`, `export_pdf`, `backtest_run`, `ticket_checked`, `trial_scan_used`, `preset_saved`, `preset_regenerated`, `contests_view_opened`, `performance_viewed`, `monthly_report_opened`, `content_shared` (only the type of shared content, never the content itself), `widget_added`, `review_prompt_shown`.
    - *Sweepstakes & referral loop:* `sweepstake_created`, `sweepstake_copied`, `sweepstake_shared`, `sweepstake_imported`, `sweepstake_auto_copied`, `sweepstake_checked`.
    - *Checking & tracking:* `ticket_entry_registered`, `auto_check_completed`, `manual_check_used`, `smart_alert_sent`, `monthly_report_sent`.
    - *Monetization:* `view_paywall`, `paywall_dismissed`, `select_premium_plan`, `purchase`, `premium_welcome_shown`.
    - *Activation & onboarding:* `onboarding_started`, `onboarding_step`, `onboarding_completed`, `notification_permission_granted`, `journey_step_completed`, `journey_dismissed`, `journey_finished`, and equivalent `premium_journey_*` events.
    - *Infrastructure & backup:* `sync_complete`, `sync_error`, `backup_started`, `backup_completed`, `backup_failed`, `restore_completed`.
- **Monetary values:** Some checking events (`auto_check_completed`, `sweepstake_checked`, `monthly_report_sent`) include the **total checked prize value** in that operation, and purchase events include the amount paid for the subscription. These are aggregate per-event values linked only to the installation's pseudonymous ID — **no numbers, no wagered amounts, and no personal identification**. They serve to measure whether checking features deliver real value to users.
- **Collection limits:** Backup events record only anonymous metadata (e.g., total item count and whether the operation was manual or automatic) — **never the content of games, sweepstakes, or preferences**. **No selected numbers, no saved tickets, no participant names, and no personally identifiable data is transmitted.**
- **Technical identifier:** Firebase Analytics uses a pseudonymous `app_instance_id` per installation, which does not allow identifying the user individually. This identifier can be reset at any time by uninstalling and reinstalling the application. The app also records two equally pseudonymous segmentation properties: whether the installation is free or Premium and which plan type (monthly, annual, or lifetime).
- **How to disable:** Collection can be turned off at any time in **About → Notifications → Privacy**. The choice is persisted on the device and applied on app launch, stopping the transmission of all events described above.
- **Advertising ID:** Collection of the Advertising ID (GAID) is explicitly **disabled** in the app's code. LottoExpert does not display ads and does not integrate any third-party advertising SDKs.
- **Legal basis (LGPD):** Developer's legitimate interest (Art. 7, IX, LGPD) for product improvement and self-campaign optimization, with data processed in an aggregate and pseudonymous manner, without disproportionate impact on the user.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 4.8 Sharing Links and QR Codes (Sweepstakes)

- **Purpose:** Share sweepstake data between users via link, QR code, or settlement statement image.
- **Operation:** The app generates a link (and optionally a QR code) containing the sweepstake data encoded via GZIP and Base64, without passing through the developer's servers.
- **Privacy:** The developer does not have access to the generated links or QR codes. Since the data is contained within the URL itself, the security of sharing depends on the user's caution when sending the link or image to third parties.
- **Settlement statement:** After the draw, the organizer can generate an image with the sweepstake's result and the division of the prize among the participants. The image is generated on the device itself and sent through the Android share menu to the destination the user chooses, without passing through the developer's servers. Participant names appear in the image by default; the organizer can turn them off before sending, in which case the image shows only "Participant 1", "Participant 2", and so on.

### 4.9 Google Drive Backup (Google) — Optional Premium Feature

- **Purpose:** Allow Premium users to save and restore their own application data (saved games, sweepstakes, and preferences) on **their personal Google Drive account**, for recovery in case of device change or loss.
- **Optional Nature:** The feature is **disabled by default** and only operates when the user **explicitly authorizes** access to Drive via the Google OAuth consent screen. No data is sent to Drive before this authorization.
- **Data Sent:** A copy of your **saved games**, **sweepstakes** (including participant names and quotas), **participations per draw** (game-draw link, with recorded cost and checked prize), **monitored numbers**, **saved generation strategies**, and app **usage preferences**, serialized in JSON format and compressed (GZIP).
- **Where Stored:** Exclusively in the hidden and private application folder (`appDataFolder`) of the **user's own Google Drive**. This folder is isolated: it is only accessible by this app, does not appear in the user's normal Drive file list, and **is not visible or accessible to other apps or other users**.
- **Developer Access:** **None.** Data travels directly between your device and your Google account. The developer **does not receive, store, or have any access** to the backup content. The app only uses the `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata` scope, which **restricts access solely to the app's own data folder** — the app cannot read or modify any other files in your Drive.
- **Automatic Backup:** If enabled by the user, the app can perform periodic backups in the background, reusing the authorization already granted. The user can disable automatic backup at any time in the feature settings.
- **Restore:** When restoring a backup on a device that already has data, the existing games and sweepstakes are preserved and those from the backup that are not yet on the device are added — restoring does not overwrite existing games or sweepstakes. If usage metrics collection (Section 4.7) is disabled in the backup, it is also disabled on the device; restoring never re-enables this collection.
- **How to Revoke and Delete:** The user can revoke the app's access to Drive at any time at [myaccount.google.com/connections](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) (Security → Third-party access). Backup data can be deleted by managing hidden application data in Google Drive settings. Uninstalling the application does **not** delete the backup: it remains in your Google account, precisely to allow restoring on a new device or after reinstalling the app.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google Drive Terms of Service](https://www.google.com/drive/terms-of-service/).

### 4.10 Google Play Subscription Notifications (RTDN)

- **Purpose:** Measure, in aggregate form, average duration and cancellation rate of Premium subscriptions to guide product and pricing decisions.
- **Operation:** When a subscription status changes (purchase, renewal, cancellation, grace period, expiration), Google Play sends an automated notification to a developer backend service hosted on Google Cloud Platform. **This flow occurs entirely between Google Play and the server — the app installed on your device does not participate or send anything.**
- **Data received:** App identifier, event type (e.g., "subscription renewed", "subscription canceled"), and time of occurrence. The purchase token provided by Google is converted into an **irreversible hash code** prior to logging so that it cannot be reconstructed. **No name, email, payment data, or user identification is received** — Google Play does not supply user identity in this flow.
- **Storage:** Events are logged strictly as technical logs in Google Cloud Logging with no database storage, no cross-referencing with app data, and no automated actions on user accounts. Logs follow standard Cloud Logging 30-day retention and are then discarded.
- **Legal basis (LGPD):** Developer's legitimate interest (Art. 7, IX) for service management and sustainability, with pseudonymized data and no impact on the data subject.
- **Google Policy:** Subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/).

## 6. Image Processing (OCR)

The "Check Ticket" feature uses the device's camera and **ML Kit Text Recognition** (Google) to identify numbers on a photographed physical ticket. In the case of Lotomania, whose playslip uses a font unrecognized by ML Kit, the app employs a proprietary reader built by the developer — which also operates **entirely on the device**.

- OCR processing occurs **entirely on the device** — both ML Kit and the proprietary reader operate offline, without sending images to Google or developer servers.
- Captured images **are not saved** in any location (gallery, cache, or database).
- Only the numbers identified in the text are used, discarding the image immediately after processing.

## 8. Data Security

- All data stored locally is in the app's private directory, protected by the Android sandbox.
- Requests to the developer's infrastructure (GCP) are authenticated via Firebase App Check and transmitted exclusively via HTTPS.
- The GCP data bucket has public access blocked—only the developer's sync service can write, and only the read service authenticated by App Check can serve data to the app.
- We use Firebase Analytics exclusively with pseudonymous and aggregate events for product improvement purposes, without collecting Advertising IDs or user identifiers (see Section 4.7), with an opt-out setting inside the app.
- Subscription notifications received from Google Play (Section 4.10) have purchase tokens hashed irreversibly before logging, keeping technical logs for a 30-day retention period.
- The optional backup on Google Drive (Section 4.9) travels via HTTPS directly between the user's device and their own Google account, using the restricted `drive.appdata` scope, without developer intervention or access.
- We do not use advertising SDKs or behavior trackers for commercial purposes.

## 9. General Data Protection Law (LGPD)

LottoExpert fully complies with the Brazilian **General Data Protection Law (Law No. 13,709/2018 - LGPD)**. This section describes the legal bases used for each type of data processing and the rights guaranteed to the data subject.

### 9.1 Legal Bases

| Processing Activity | LGPD Legal Basis |
| :--- | :--- |
| Draw results synchronization (GCP) | Contract execution / legitimate interest — Art. 7, V and IX |
| Firebase App Check (integrity attestation) | Legitimate interest — protection against abuse of infrastructure — Art. 7, IX |
| Google Play Billing (purchases and subscriptions) | Contract execution — Art. 7, V |
| Firebase Crashlytics (crash diagnostics) | Legitimate interest — maintenance and improvement of service — Art. 7, IX |
| Firebase Analytics (aggregate app usage) | Legitimate interest — product improvement and self-campaign optimization — Art. 7, IX |
| Firebase Remote Config (bet prices) | Legitimate interest — Art. 7, IX |
| Google Play subscription notifications — RTDN (Section 4.10) | Legitimate interest — service management and sustainability, with pseudonymized data — Art. 7, IX |
| User backup on Google Drive (Premium, optional) | Consent — Art. 7, I (explicit OAuth authorization, revocable at any time) |
| Local data (games, sweepstakes, preferences) | Contract execution / implied consent by usage — Art. 7, V |

### 9.2 Data Subject Rights

As a data subject, you have the following rights guaranteed by the LGPD (Art. 18):

- **Confirmation and access:** confirm if your data is processed and access it.
- **Correction:** request the correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data.
- **Deletion:** request the deletion of data processed based on consent.
- **Portability:** request the portability of data to another service provider.
- **Information:** be informed about the entities with which your data is shared.
- **Revocation of consent:** revoke consent at any time.
- **Objection:** object to processing carried out based on legitimate interest when it causes a disproportionate impact.

**How to exercise your rights:** contact us via the official support channel on the application's page in the Google Play Store. We will reply within 15 business days.

**Local data:** to delete data stored on the device (saved games, sweepstakes, preferences), use the deletion options inside the app itself or uninstall the application.

### 9.3 International Data Transfer

Firebase services (Analytics, Crashlytics, App Check, Remote Config), Google Cloud Platform, and Google Drive (optional backup in the user's account) are operated by Google, with servers located outside Brazil. These transfers are carried out based on Google's Standard Contractual Clauses, which provide safeguards equivalent to those required by the LGPD (Art. 33, II). Please refer to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.
