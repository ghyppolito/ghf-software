## 2. Description of the service

## 4. Important safety notice — observing the Sun

**Never look directly at the Sun**, including during a partial or annular solar eclipse or during the partial phases of a total eclipse, without adequate and certified eye protection (for example, eclipse glasses compliant with the **ISO 12312-2** standard). Ordinary sunglasses, photographic film, X-ray film or smoked glass are **not** safe protection. Never point cameras, binoculars or telescopes at the Sun without an appropriate solar filter.

Improper observation of the Sun can cause **serious and permanent eye injury, including blindness**. EclipseGo only provides information about the events and is **not** a safety device or a safety guide for observation. Responsibility for safe observation lies solely with the user.
