## 2. What TopoWalk Is

TopoWalk is an Android app that **supports field topographic surveying**. It captures points using the device's GPS, the barometer or, optionally, an external GNSS/RTK receiver connected via Bluetooth, and it calculates distances, height differences, slopes, azimuths and areas. It also generates PDF reports and exports surveys in commonly used file formats.

The app has a free part and a paid part, **TopoWalk Pro** (section 5).

## 3. Accuracy and Technical Responsibility

**Read this section carefully. It defines what TopoWalk is and what it is not.**

- **The results are estimates.** Accuracy depends on the device, the receiver, the visible sky, vegetation, nearby buildings, atmospheric conditions and how the app is used. With a phone's internal GPS, the typical error is a few metres. The app shows the accuracy reported by the system or by the receiver, but it cannot guarantee it.
- **TopoWalk is not a certified or calibrated measuring instrument.** It does not replace approved surveying equipment or the work of a legally qualified professional.
- **On its own, it is not fit for legal or land-registry purposes.** Georeferencing of properties for certification (for example, in SIGEF/INCRA, the Brazilian federal land-registry georeferencing system), area rectification, boundary demarcation, expert surveys, engineering projects and any purpose that the law reserves for a qualified professional require that professional, with the corresponding technical responsibility (in Brazil, the ART or RRT, the Brazilian professional technical-responsibility records).
