# Privacy Policy — Pole Position: Race Analytics

**Last updated:** October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Pole Position: Race Analytics** application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects user information ("you"). By installing and using the App, you agree to the terms of this policy.

---

## 1. About the App

**Pole Position: Race Analytics** is a sports telemetry Android app developed for karting and amateur motorsports. The App utilizes GPS and motion sensors to record laps, calculate lap times, measure lateral G-forces, and provide on-track performance analysis.

---

## Data Safety Summary (Google Play)

This summary reflects the information declared in the **Data Safety** section of the App's store listing on the Google Play Store. According to Google's definition, **data processed exclusively on the device and never transmitted outside of it is not considered "collected" or "shared"**.

### Data collected (sent outside the device)

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Purpose | Optional collection |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approximate location (derived from IP by Firebase) | Yes | No | Analytics | No |
| App activity (interactions and usage events) | Yes | No | Analytics | No |
| IDs (App instance ID generated by Firebase) | Yes | No | Analytics | No |
| Crash logs and diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics) | Yes | No | Analytics and error fixing | No |

### Data processed only on the device (not declared as collected)

The following data **never leaves the device** and, therefore, is not declared as collected or shared in the store listing — except when the user chooses to manually export or share it (see sections 5 and 8):

- **Precise location (GPS)** and **lap trace** (sequence of coordinates) — stored only in the local database.
- **Motion sensor data** (lateral and longitudinal G-forces).
- **User-entered content** (track names, session notes, lap and split times).

### Security practices

- **Data is encrypted in transit** (HTTPS connection to Firebase — Analytics and Crashlytics).
- **You can request the deletion of your data:** local data is removed when uninstalling the App or by deleting them individually in the App's screens; analytics data can be cleared by resetting the device's advertising identifier (see section 8).
- **No data is sold or shared with third parties** for their own purposes.
- The App **does not collect personally identifiable information** (name, email, phone number, financial data).

## 2. Collected Data

### 2.1 Location Data (GPS)

The App requests precise location permission (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`) to:

- Detect finish line and sector line crossings with millisecond precision.
- Calculate real-time vehicle speed (km/h) based on GPS data.
- Center the map on your current position during track configuration.
- Record the **best lap trace** (sequence of GPS coordinates sampled during the session), when the user enables this feature for a track (see section 2.5).

On Android 12 or higher, the system requires the precise location request to come together with the **approximate location** request (`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`), and lets the user choose between the two. The App only times laps with precise location: if only approximate location is granted, timing does not work and the App asks for precise location again. The approximate location permission is not used for any other purpose and is not to be confused with the approximate location (country) that Firebase Analytics derives from the IP address, described above.

Location data is used **exclusively inside the device**. No GPS coordinates are ever transmitted to external servers, third parties, or any cloud services.

### 2.2 Motion Sensor Data

The App accesses the device's **accelerometer** and **gyroscope** to:

- Measure lateral G-forces during driving.
- Display real-time acceleration data on the dashboard.

Sensor data is processed locally in memory. Lateral and longitudinal G-force readings **are not transmitted externally**. They are persisted locally only as part of the best lap trace, when the user enables this feature for a track (see section 2.5); outside this feature, no raw sensor data is written to the database.

### 2.3 User-Input Data

The App stores locally on the device:

- **Track names** and coordinates of finish and sector lines registered by the user.
- **Lap times** and splits recorded during sessions.
- **Session notes** entered voluntarily by the user after each session.
- **Maximum and minimum speeds** and **maximum G-force** per lap.

All of this data is stored exclusively in the local database of the device (Room/SQLite) and never leaves the device, except when the user manually chooses to export it.

### 2.4 Automatically Collected Usage Data (Firebase Analytics)

The App integrates **Firebase Analytics** (Google LLC) for anonymous usage metrics collection. Collected data includes:

- **App behavior events:** user flows (onboarding, track creation, starting and ending sessions, session analysis), configuration actions (theme changes, screen orientation, TTS) and history actions. Sessions and tracks appear only as internal numeric identifiers — the sequential number of the record in the local database, which does not identify the user —, with no name or location. Three activation milestones are sent only once per installation, with no parameters: first track created, first and second session with a recorded lap.
- **Timing technical signals:** a count of how many line crossings were discarded by the lap timer's guards, by closed-category reason, sent at the end of the session. It does not include location, track, or time.
- **Session technical signals**, with numeric and closed-category values only:
  - session end: lap count, **total session duration** (from start to end) and whether the session was ended automatically;
  - session discarded with no saved record: reason, lap count and total session duration;
  - telemetry service terminated by the system: lap count, total session duration and when it was detected;
  - GPS signal loss: **duration without GPS signal**, lap number and whether the signal came back.

  These are durations of the whole session or of the interval without signal, never lap or sector times; on an open track ended automatically at the end of the run, the total session duration adds up the run and the time before the start, and is not the run time. The failure signals are also sent to Crashlytics (see section 2.6).
- **Aggregated user properties:** theme mode, active TTS mode, total tracks created and activation stage (none, track created, one or two sessions with a recorded lap).
- **Technical device data:** automatically collected by Firebase, including device model, Android version, system language, and country of origin (derived from IP, not stored as raw data).
- **Install source:** Firebase reads from the Google Play Store where the installation came from (for example, the link or the campaign of the App's store page). It is acquisition-channel information, sent along with the other usage data and associated, like them, with the Firebase instance identifier. It does not include the advertising ID.

**What is NOT collected via analytics:**
- GPS coordinates or real-time geographic location.
- Performance and driving data (lap times, speeds, G-force).
- Track names, session notes, or any user-entered content.
- Data that allows individual identification of the user (name, email, ID, etc.).

This data is used exclusively to understand which features are used and improve the App in future versions. There is no selling or sharing of this information for advertising purposes. The App **does not use the Android advertising ID** or the Privacy Sandbox advertising and ad attribution APIs, and advertising ID collection by Firebase Analytics is disabled.

### 2.5 Lap Trace (GPS) — Optional Feature

The App offers a **trace analysis** feature that records the sequence of GPS coordinates of the best lap of each session to generate speed, G-force graphs, and the track outline. Regarding this feature:

- It is **strictly optional and opt-in**: recording only occurs after the user explicitly enables tracing for a specific track. By default, it is **disabled**.
- The recorded points (GPS coordinate, speed, and G-force at each instant) are stored **exclusively in the device's local database** and are never sent to external servers.
- Retention is limited: the App keeps the trace of a maximum of **3 sessions per track**, automatically removing the oldest ones to free up space.
- The user can disable the feature at any time and delete the stored traces (see section 8).

### 2.6 Crash Reports and Diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics)

The App integrates **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google LLC) to identify crashes and operational issues. The following are sent:

- **Crash reports and non-fatal errors**, including stack traces of the App's code.
- **Environmental failure modes tracked by the App**, without which issues that do not trigger a crash would remain invisible: loss of GPS signal during a session, OS termination of the telemetry service (typical of aggressive battery optimizations), and started sessions that produced no saved record. Accompanied only by closed-category and numeric values — **total session duration** (from start to end), **duration without GPS signal**, lap number, lap count, discard reason and whether the GPS signal came back.
- **Technical device data and App state** automatically collected by Crashlytics: model, Android version, available memory and storage, and an installation ID generated by Crashlytics itself.

**What is NOT sent to Crashlytics:**
- GPS coordinates or any location data.
- Lap times, speeds, G-forces, or any driving performance data.
- Track names, notes, or user-entered content.
- Data allowing individual identification of the user.

### 2.7 Data NOT Collected

The App **does not collect, store, or transmit**:

- Name, email, phone number, or any personal identification data.
- Social media account info or third-party accounts.
- Payment information.
- Browsing history or data from other apps.
- Location or telemetry data outside the context of an active session.

## 3. Use of Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` | Precise line crossing detection and GPS speed calculations |
| `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` | Required by Android, together with precise location, to request the permission (Android 12+). Used only on the device; does not enable timing on its own |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Runs the telemetry service in the foreground with a visible notification banner |
| `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION` | Location classification requirement for Android 14+ systems |
| `WAKE_LOCK` | Prevents the CPU from sleeping during telemetry sessions to guarantee continuous capture |
| `HIGH_SAMPLING_RATE_SENSORS` | High-frequency access to accelerometer and gyroscope readings |
| `ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY` | Suppress external notifications during the session when the user enables this option |
| `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Display the telemetry service notification, with the session status and the stop-recording button (Android 13+). It does not grant access to any data; if denied, the session works normally |
| `INTERNET` | Sends anonymous events to Firebase Analytics, sends crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics, and fetches map layers from Google Maps API |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Check for a network connection before sending events to Firebase and loading the map |
| `BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE` | Read from the Google Play Store the install source (link or campaign), sent to Firebase Analytics along with the usage data. It is not an identifier and does not grant access to any user data |

The App does **not** use background location (`ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`). During a racing session started by the user, the telemetry service runs in the foreground, with a visible notification and a button to stop recording; that is why it keeps recording with the screen off or the App minimized. Outside an active session, the App only accesses location while the track configuration screen is open.

## 4. Storage and Security

- Telemetry data (tracks, sessions, laps) is stored exclusively on the user's device.
- The App does not have its own server for driving data and does not send it to any external server.
- Anonymous app usage data is sent to Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) via encrypted HTTPS connections. This data is managed according to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- Crash reports and diagnostics are sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) via the same encrypted connection, as described in section 2.6.
- Local data security depends on Android's native OS protections and the lock screen security of the user's device.
- Uninstalling the App removes all data stored locally on the device.

## 5. Data Sharing

The App does not automatically share driving data with third parties.

The sharing mechanisms for session data are always **manually triggered by the user** and the content is delivered to the destination app chosen by them (email, messaging app, cloud storage, etc.). They are:

- **CSV Export**, triggered by the share button on the session analysis screen. The generated file contains only the telemetry data of the selected session.
- **Analysis Card (PNG image)**, generated on the lap trace screen. The image is built entirely on the device and contains the track outline (a normalized shape, derived from the GPS trace, without absolute geographic coordinates), performance graphs (speed, G-force), and a QR code pointing to the App's page on the Google Play Store.

In both cases, the App **does not retain a copy** of the file after sharing and no data is sent to the developer's servers.

Anonymous usage data (Firebase Analytics events) and crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are processed by Google as described in section 6.

## 8. User Rights

As your driving data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it:

- **Access:** Data can be viewed directly on the App's screens.
- **Export:** Data can be exported to CSV at any time.
- **Partial Deletion:** Laps, sessions, and tracks can be deleted individually within the App.
- **GPS Trace Control:** Lap trace recording is opt-in per track and can be disabled at any time; already stored traces can be removed by the App.
- **Total Deletion:** Uninstalling the App removes all data stored locally.

For data collected by Firebase Analytics, you can opt out of analytics tracking in your Android settings under **Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization** or through Google Play Services privacy options.

Firebase Crashlytics crash reports are not linked to your identity and use an installation ID generated by the service itself. This identifier is discarded when uninstalling the App.
